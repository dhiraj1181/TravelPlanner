    Under the Hood

    Real Algorithms,
    Not Just Templates

    Most trip planners just show you a list. TravelPro solves the Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP) — the same mathematical problem used by logistics firms to optimise delivery routes — applied to your day's sightseeing.

    Our ML pipeline fetches real Points of Interest from OpenStreetMap, ranks them by quality scores and your personal history, clusters them geographically by day, then runs a 2-opt TSP optimisation to minimise travel time within each day.

    🗺️ OpenStreetMap POIs 🧮 TSP 2-opt Routing 📊 Budget ML Prediction 🔁 Personalised History ⚡ FastAPI + Spring Boot
